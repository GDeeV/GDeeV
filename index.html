About Me

I'm a technology reporter at The Washington Post, where I focus on the intersection of technology, national security, and artificial intelligence. My reporting explores how emerging technologies are reshaping military capabilities, defense policy, and global security dynamics.

My work examines critical questions about the development and deployment of AI systems in military contexts, the evolving relationship between Silicon Valley and the defense establishment, and the broader implications of technological change for society and security.
